IPE® Technology

Iperen IPE® Liquid 3 - 15 - 0

Application

Agronomical target

  • NUE
  • Nutrition

Certifications

Iperen IPE® Liquid | 3 - 15 - 0 is a biostimulant especially suitable during the first half of the crop cycle, releasing soil-bound Phosphates and preventing the precipitation of newly applied Phosphates. As a result, the level of soil plant-available phosphate increases, resulting in better overall plant performance, as shown in many trials. Thanks to IPE® Technology the input of freshly applied phosphates can be reduced due to the higher Nutrient Use Efficiency (NUE). The product is a stable solution, compatible with all phosphate-based straight fertilizers (e.g. MAP, MKP, UP).

  • Increases NUE (Nutrient Use Efficiency) in both alkaline and acidic soils
  • Boosts general plant performances
  • Developed for fertigation in open field crops
  • Improves root and shoot growth, for high quality buds, and boosts general plant performance
