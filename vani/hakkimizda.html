About us

Van Iperen International is a Dutch producer of Specialty Fertilizers and Biostimulants developed for high-performing applications. We are eager to change the rules of the game in plant nutrition with the widest portfolio of solutions that bring innovation, drive performance, and empower growers for a greater impact. We call it the 'Green Switch Journey'—a dynamic roadmap to make conventional agriculture more sustainable.

"Nowadays, we are facing the challenge of making the switch to a greener future. Innovation, performance, empowerment, and resilience will lead the way. Here’s how—and why."

Erik van den Bergh
Managing Director Van Iperen International

We Innovate

Innovation drives change. At Van Iperen International, we develop and implement solutions that reduce the carbon footprint of agriculture and optimize resources while ensuring high yields and crop quality. Through:

  • Low Carbon Footprint Solutions: Minimizing the environmental impact of agriculture activities by supplying essential nutrients with a low carbon footprint. These solutions are part of our GreenSwitch® range.
  • Proactive Crop Management Solutions: Enabling growers to proactively addresses various stress and physiological factors. These solutions are part of Plants for Plants® range.

These innovative solutions are designed for an easy-adaption and integration into local farming practices for a quick impact towards more sustainability.

We Perform

Performance is about both our solutions and how they are applied:

  • High-Performing Solutions maximize agricultural inputs by improving Nutrient Use Efficiency (NUE) and Water Use Efficiency (WUE) while elevating Abiotic Stress Tolerance.
  • High-Performing Applications ensure fertilizers and biostimulants are applied in the right way, at the right time, and in the right place—whether through fertigation or foliar application—delivering nutrients precisely where they are needed for optimal plant performance, resource efficiency, and sustainability.

By improving the way water and nutrients are used, we help growers achieve greater yields and higher crop quality—without compromising sustainability.

We Empower

Empower is about sharing our knowledge for greater impact. At Van Iperen International, we believe that when our customers grow, we grow together.

  • Our agronomists provide hands-on training and tailored advice in the field, helping growers and distributors make the most of our solutions.
  • Through clear, locally adapted content and digital tools, our marketing team makes agronomy easy to access and apply.

By making knowledge accessible and actionable, we help our partners perform better—today and tomorrow.

We are Resilient

Being resilient means adapting to a changing world. It’s about moving forward—despite pressure, complexity, or uncertainty.

  • We see sustainability as a journey. We act today while continuously improving for tomorrow.
  • We put transparency into practice and turn ideas into real actions—across products, operations, and partnerships.
  • Our strength lies in being close to the field, listening, and adjusting with agility.

We believe resilience is built over time—through consistent action, human connection, and a clear purpose.
