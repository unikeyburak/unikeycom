Biostimulants

At the forefront of agricultural innovation, our Biostimulants are developed with a focus on maximizing the efficiency of agricultural inputs. By significantly improving Nutrient Use Efficiency (NUE) and Water Use Efficiency (WUE), we help growers get more from less and boost plants' natural resilience. Our Biostimulants, applied using High-Performing Applications, enhance crops' Abiotic Stress Tolerance, promoting superior Crop Quality and boosting sustainable practices, even under challenging environmental conditions. This portfolio also includes Plants for Plants® (P4P), our range of plant-based biostimulants. Acting as Proactive Crop Management tools, P4P makes crops better equipped to handle stress and thrive.

Essentials

Produced from pure and carefully selected raw materials, our Essentials range provides high-quality primary crop nutrition. These water-soluble and liquid fertilizers deliver the essential nutrients for robust plant development and growth, ensuring a superior and qualitative crop yield. Our solutions are delivered precisely where the plant needs them most, utilizing high-performance applications. This portfolio also includes GreenSwitch®, our innovative range of circular fertilizers with low carbon footprint emissions, providing growers with premium crop nutrition while reducing their environmental impact.
